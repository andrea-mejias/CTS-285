# Robot Makes a Sandwich
This is a markdown document containing instructions of how to teach a robot to make a sandwich from start to finish.

# Assumptions
The starting state is bread, a jar of peanut butter, and a knife. It must be on the counter. The bread is already out. Pretty sure it is sliced bread in a bag. The robot's hands are empty. 

# Steps

- Step 1
Grab two slices of bread from the sliced bread bag.

- Step 2
Close the sliced bread bag and put it to the side of the counter.

- Step 3
Put the two slices flat on the table separately.

- Step 4
Remove the lid from the jar of peanut butter. 

- Step 5
Put the lid beside the jar.

- Step 6
Grab the knife and keep holding onto it.

- Step 7
Use the knife to scoop up a fair amount of peanut butter from the jar.

- Step 8
Take out the knife with the scooped peanut butter.

- Step 9
Spread the knife onto the face of the first slice until all the peanut butter is off the knife.

- Step 10
Put the knife on the counter beside the jar.

- Step 11
Grab the second slice from the counter and stack it on top of the first slice.

- Step 12
Close the jar of peanut butter with the lid.

# Done When
the jar is closed with the lid. Grab your sandwich and serve it to someone you know or for yourself.
