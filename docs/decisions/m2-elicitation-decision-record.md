# M2 Elicitation Decision Record

## Investigation Path
1. Which original DataMan behaviors are considered essential to preserve?
   Evidence revealed: Stakeholders identify immediate answer feedback, repeated practice after an incorrect response, and a clear way for learners to see progress as central to the original experience.
2. What do parents or teachers need to understand about learner activity?
   Evidence revealed: Adults want to understand what the learner practiced and whether progress is occurring, but stakeholders have not yet agreed on a detailed reporting dashboard.
3. What exactly do stakeholders mean by “students shouldn’t lose their work”?
   Evidence revealed: Teachers report that students may pause practice and return later. They want a learner’s saved practice state to remain available after leaving and returning to the application.

## Initial Position
**Supported evidence:**
I now know that stakeholders want DataMan to be modern and simple so that students will not lose their work and parents will continue to understand what their child is doing.

**Remaining uncertainty:**
What is uncertain is if a student's work will be kept protected or encrypted, and if the dashboard criterion will pass through.

**Likely functional requirement:**
A session must stay at the previous state it was left from if anything comes up in the student's time and will have to leave the program.

**Likely non-functional requirement / quality constraint:**
Students can be on a session for long periods of time.

**Assumption or proposed solution I am not treating as confirmed:**
The website must be as fast as the original DataMan to help students go through answers smoothly.

**Why my initial position is defensible:**
It is defensible because there are still some approaches that need refinement or may have to be deferred. Although some will move forward, it is important to define the rest before doing the next decision.

## Complication
Students may use DataMan on school Chromebooks, phones, tablets, and home computers. Some sessions may be interrupted before intentional sign-out.

**What this affects:**
This new information will affect the session approach as certain devices may not save a students' session due to signing out.

**What I revised, if anything:**
I can revise the account approach to state of saving session data into an account so that their work will stay intact if certain users have to sign out.

**Final decision and reasoning:**
Revise assumptions as this change gives me more information to make a better decision on how each requirement will go.
